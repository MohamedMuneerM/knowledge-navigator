# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help people learn. Treat everyone here the way a good teacher treats
a curious student.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. There are no stupid questions here.
- Disagree about content with evidence (curricula, textbooks, papers), not with personal remarks.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately adding false or misleading content.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@MohamedMuneerM](https://github.com/MohamedMuneerM)). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone
whose behaviour they judge harmful.
